A dedicated study guide and technical reference for professionals preparing for the **American Petroleum Institute (API) 580 Risk-Based Inspection Certification Exam**, covering RBI principles, risk assessment, equipment damage mechanisms, and inspection program development.

---

## Exam Overview

The API 580 certification validates professional knowledge of Risk-Based Inspection (RBI) principles and their application to inspection planning, risk assessment, and the management of pressure-containing equipment in the petrochemical industry.

- **Exam Format**: Computer-based, closed-book, multiple-choice examination; available at in-person test centers or through remote proctoring.
- **Target Audience**: Inspectors, engineers, and other professionals working in the petrochemical, refining, chemical processing, and related industries.
- **Prerequisites**: A current API 510, API 570, or API 653 certification qualifies candidates automatically. Otherwise, candidates must meet API's education and petrochemical-industry experience requirements.

## Core Exam Domains & Knowledge Areas

### 1. Terms, Definitions, and Acronyms

- Risk-Based Inspection terminology and fundamental concepts.
- Definitions and abbreviations used in API RP 580.
- Technical language related to equipment integrity and risk.

### 2. Risk Assessment

- Risk assessment principles and RBI analysis approaches.
- Risk evaluation, prioritization, and assessment planning.
- Understanding uncertainty and risk-based decision-making.

### 3. RBI Program

- Establishing and implementing an RBI program.
- Planning, execution, and ongoing program management.
- Integrating RBI into equipment inspection activities.

### 4. Roles, Responsibilities, Training, and Qualifications

- Responsibilities of owner-users and RBI team members.
- Competency, training, and qualification requirements.
- Collaboration among inspection, engineering, and operations personnel.

### 5. Data Collection

- Collecting equipment design, operating, and inspection information.
- Identifying data requirements and evaluating data quality.
- Reviewing information needed for risk assessment.

### 6. Damage Mechanisms

- Identifying equipment damage mechanisms and failure modes.
- Evaluating material, process, and operating conditions.
- Understanding damage mechanism reviews and inspection implications.

### 7. Probability of Failure (POF)

- Understanding probability-of-failure assessment principles.
- Evaluating equipment condition and damage-related factors.
- Using POF information in risk evaluation.

### 8. Consequence of Failure (COF)

- Understanding potential consequences of equipment failure.
- Evaluating safety, environmental, and business impacts.
- Applying consequence information to risk assessment.

### 9. Risk Management

- Determining and evaluating equipment risk.
- Prioritizing inspection and other risk mitigation activities.
- Reassessment and updating of risk management decisions.

### 10. RBI Documentation

- Maintaining RBI assessment records and documentation.
- Recording assumptions, results, and inspection recommendations.
- Supporting traceability and future reassessment.
